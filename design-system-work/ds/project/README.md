Course Planner is the design system behind **YU 수강 플래너**, a Next.js web app in which 영남대학교 students lay out courses semester by semester and track graduation requirements. It was read out of the codebase (Tailwind CSS v4 utilities, the `src/components` React components and the pages) and written down as tokens, usage rules and static renditions, so that whoever builds the next screen, admin table or marketing section matches what already ships.

The look: a serious university blue (`yu-navy`) with a cool cyan accent (`yu-cyan`), white cards on a `gray-50` frame, small quiet type, Lucide line icons, and a planner whose chips and surfaces carry meaning through fixed color pairs. Dense where students work, loud only on the landing page.

## Using this system

- **Tokens** (`tokens.json`, compiled to CSS custom properties): one light theme. Colors are Tailwind v4's own names (`gray-600`, `red-500`, `blue-100`) at their exact v4 values, plus the brand hexes the code hard-codes (`yu-navy`, `yu-blue`, `yu-cyan`, `yu-navy-hover` …), alpha steps (`yu-navy-a10`, `white-a60`), and a thin alias layer (`surface`, `ink-muted`, `line`, `focus-ring`, `danger`, and the planner's `category-*`, `status-*`, `pill-*`, `dept-*` pairs). Spacing is the 4px Tailwind scale (`space-2` = 8px, halves written `space-1-5`), radii `radius-sm` … `radius-full`, shadows `shadow-sm` … `shadow-2xl` plus the landing's `shadow-glow`, sizes for controls, icons and content columns, and durations and curves for motion. Every token's usage note says where the app uses it and names the grounds a text color reads on.
- **Type** is one family: the body stack `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` (the product overrides Geist on `body`); Geist and Geist Mono are declared for `font-sans` / `font-mono` but effectively unused. Styles are named by role: `display-*` (landing only), `heading-1…4`, `body`, `body-sm`, `body-sm-medium`, `caption`, `micro-11/10/9`, `overline`, `mono-sm`.
- **Components** are static renditions, not the React code: each card's `preview.html` is plain HTML styled by `components/bundle.css` (`.cp-<component>` classes, every value a `var(--token, literal)`), with the component's real props in `components/index.d.ts` and usage rules in its README. Build in React with the app's own components when you are inside the repository; use the CSS and markup when you are not. Card groups: Primitives (`Button`, `Card`, `Input`, `Toast`, `SearchableSelect`, `Footer`, `ZoomableImage`, `ErrorBoundary`), Planner (`CourseCard`, `SemesterColumn`, `ReadOnlySemesterColumn`, `RequirementsSummary`, `RequirementForm`, `CustomCourseForm`, `AddSemesterDialog`, `CourseCatalog`, `AllPlansModal`, `AnonymousPlanModal`, `PatchNotePreview`) and Patterns — recurring markup the app repeats without a component (`AppHeader`, `AuthLayout`, `PageHeader`, `FilterChips`, `Badge`, `StatusStates`, `Statistics`, `Onboarding`, `CalendarGrid`, `AdminList`, `FeedbackCard`, `ProfileCard`, `PatchNotes`, `HelpSection`, `ModalShell`, with `Callout` to follow).
- **Assets**: the YU wordmark in color and white (`assets/Logos`), the 57 Lucide icons the app imports (`assets/Icons`), the PWA and social icons (`assets/App Icons`), and the help-page screenshots of the real UI (`assets/Screenshots`). Each group's README says how it is used.
- **Sections** beyond this book: Voice and copy, Planner semantics, Motion, Landing page, Layout and shell.

## Content fundamentals

Korean, polite, brief, one student at a time. Three registers, chosen by what the text does: **labels** are terse nouns or -하기 forms (바로 시작하기, 더 보기, 취소); **guidance** is 해요체 imperative (과목을 드래그하세요, 졸업 요건을 설정하세요); **statements** — status, errors, confirmations, legal — are 합니다체 (문제가 발생했습니다, 정말 삭제하시겠습니까?). The reader is never 당신; ownership is 나만의. Full sentences end with a period; labels, headings, hints and validation lines do not. Exclamation marks are rare (복사됨!, one statistics banner) and emoji do not belong in the UI (the source has two strays: 🔴 on calendar holidays, ⚠️ in a terms heading). Numbers glue to their units (3학점, 1학년 1학기, 2026년, 45개) and ` · ` separates summary items. Loanwords are Hangul (드래그앤드롭, 리스트, 커스텀); English is reserved for the product name, the slogan "YU the Future", the landing eyebrows SERVICE / HOW IT WORKS, the Focus badge and the footer headings. Category and status words are fixed vocabulary (전공핵심 … 자유선택; 계획 / 수강중 / 완료 / 재수강, 예정 / 이수 in compact pills). The Voice and copy section has the full rules with the product's real strings.

## Visual foundations

### Color

The brand is three blues on white. `yu-navy` (#153974) is the identity color: page titles' accent words, links, active nav items, the Focus badge, and solid fills that always carry white text. `yu-blue` (#3069b3) is its companion: the second stop of every brand gradient (`linear-gradient(to right in oklab, yu-navy, yu-blue)` on the primary Button; `to bottom right` on avatars and hero panels), the info Toast rail and the "before" bar of progress deltas. `yu-cyan` (#00aaca) is the accent: focus rings, the curriculum-year badge, help-link color, decorative dots and rings, and the last stop of the footer's gradient bar — never body text (2.8:1 on white). Hover darkens: `yu-navy` → `yu-navy-hover`, gradients shift `yu-navy-hover → yu-navy`. The landing page and auth brand panel go deeper with `yu-navy-deepest` → `yu-navy-dark` gradients; the footer sits on `footer-navy`.

Neutrals are Tailwind gray on the dashboard and admin surfaces, Tailwind slate on the planner, requirements and admin tables; the app mixes them and so may you, but keep one family within a component. The frame behind cards is `gray-50`; cards, panels, inputs and modals are `white`. Text steps: `gray-900` titles, `gray-800` strong copy and course names, `gray-700` body and labels, `gray-600` secondary copy, `gray-500` helper text (4.8:1 — the floor for body text), `gray-400` placeholders and resting icons only (2.6:1). The same ladder exists in slate (`slate-700` names, `slate-600` body, `slate-500` meta, `slate-400` icons).

Status colors are Tailwind families used as fixed pairs: a 50 or 100 tint behind 700 (or 800) text, a 500 or 600 solid under white. Danger is red (`red-600` fills, `red-500` validation text and error borders, `red-50`/`red-200` boxes); warning is amber (`amber-50` surfaces, `amber-200` borders, `amber-800` text, `amber-500` rail); success is green for enrollment and emerald for completion and positive stats; info is `blue-50` behind `blue-700`. Requirement categories, course statuses and catalog filters have their own fixed maps — see the Planner semantics section and use the `category-*`, `status-*`, `pill-*` and `dept-*` aliases so a chip's meaning travels with its color.

Known pairs that miss 4.5:1 and are kept from the source, flagged in their token notes: `red-500` text on white (3.8:1), `amber-600` and `orange-600` on white, `emerald-600` on white (3.5:1, used only beside 18px+ bold stats), `gray-400` and `slate-400` as readable text, white on the 500-step filter chips (orange, green, emerald, gray), `gray-500` fine print on `footer-navy`, and the `yu-cyan` focus ring on white (2.8:1). Prefer `red-600`, `amber-700`, `gray-500` and `yu-blue` in new work.

### Type

One family: the body stack is `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` (globals.css sets it on `body` after Tailwind's preflight, overriding Geist). Geist and Geist Mono are loaded through `next/font` and exposed as `font-sans` / `font-mono`, but no element opts into `font-sans`; `font-mono` appears once, on admin user IDs. Korean text therefore falls back to the OS Korean sans (Malgun Gothic, Apple SD Gothic Neo, Noto Sans CJK); do not add a webfont to "fix" this — it is the product's look.

Sizes are Tailwind's: `body-sm` (14/20) is the UI default for inputs, rows, labels and buttons, at 500 weight for anything clickable (`body-sm-medium`); `caption` (12/16) for meta, helper text and chips; `body` (16/24) only for prose, with `body-relaxed` (1.625) on help and legal pages. Headings: `heading-2` (24/32, 700) is the dashboard page title; `heading-3` (20/28, 600) card and modal titles; `heading-4` (18/28, 600) section titles in cards and semester columns; `heading-1` (30/36, 700) the onboarding title and landing section titles on mobile. Display sizes 36–72px, bold with `tracking-tight`, belong to the landing hero only. Below 12px the planner uses `micro-11`, `micro-10` and `micro-9` for chips, credit counts and the compact status pill — keep them for dense planner rows and never for required reading. Uppercase tracked eyebrows (`overline`, 0.1em) mark landing sections and footer column headings.

### Spacing and layout

A 4px grid. Inline rhythm is `space-2` (8px gap) between items and `space-1-5` inside chips; `space-3` × `space-2` pads inputs and small buttons (12 × 8); `space-4` × `space-2` the default Button; cards pad `space-4` (compact), `space-5` or `space-6` (Card parts); modals pad `space-6` horizontally and `space-4` vertically in headers, `space-6` or `space-8` in bodies. Vertical rhythm between blocks is `space-4` (16), between sections `space-6` (24), from page header to content `space-8` (32). Landing sections breathe at `space-16`–`space-32`.

Content columns: `container-7xl` (1280) for the nav and planner frame, `container-6xl` (1152) for dashboard pages, `container-5xl` (1024) for landing content, `container-2xl` (672) for help, patch notes and profile, `container-md` (448) for auth and small modals, `container-4xl` (896) for the plan modals. Side gutters are `space-4` on mobile, `space-6`–`space-8` from `md`. Breakpoints are Tailwind's (`sm` 640, `md` 768, `lg` 1024); the app designs mobile-first and switches most layouts at `md` (128 uses) with a lighter `sm` tier (68).

### Shape, borders and elevation

Radii: `radius-lg` (8px) is the container radius — Card, Toast, banners, list rows; `radius-md` (6px) for controls — Button, Input, selects, segmented buttons; `radius-sm` (4px) for chips; `radius-xl` (12px) for modal panels and stat tiles; `radius-2xl` (16px) for landing cards and the auth panel; `radius-full` for pills, avatars, progress bars and icon buttons; `radius-xs` (2px) for legend swatches.

Borders are 1px. Note what Tailwind v4 does with a bare `border`: it draws in `currentColor`, so Card, the semester columns, the default status pill and several `border-t` dividers really are `gray-900` hairlines — visibly dark, and part of the product's look (`border-default`). Where a light line is intended the source says so: `slate-200` on planner cards and tables, `gray-200` on Toast and catalog panels, `gray-300` on inputs, `gray-100` on read-only rows, `border-2 dashed gray-200` for drop zones, `border-2` solid for segmented buttons and selectable plan cards. Colored 4px rails (`border-l-4`) mark Toast type and satisfied requirement rows; `border-t-4` marks patch-note cards.

Shadows are quiet: `shadow-sm` at rest on Card and the primary Button, `shadow-md` on hover, `shadow-lg` on floating menus and Toast, `shadow-xl` on modal panels and drag overlays, `shadow-2xl` on the auth panel and the hero screenshot. The landing adds a navy `shadow-glow` under its CTA and a lifted `shadow-card-hover` on feature cards. The glass utilities (`.glass`, `.glass-strong`: white at 70% / 85% with 12px / 20px blur) exist for the landing only.

### States and focus

Hover: fills step one shade (`gray-100` → `gray-200`, `yu-navy` → `yu-navy-hover`), outline buttons wash `gray-50`, rows wash `gray-50` or `slate-100`, links turn `yu-cyan` (footer) or `yu-navy` (help links), icon buttons go `gray-400` → `gray-600`, or `red-500` / `amber-500` for destructive and reset actions. Active: Button scales to 0.98. Disabled: `opacity-50` with `pointer-events: none` (Button) or `cursor-not-allowed` (Input, segmented buttons); catalog items already in the plan drop to `opacity-60` or `opacity-50`.

Focus comes in three recipes, all `outline: none` plus a ring: Input and SearchableSelect use a solid 2px `focus-ring` (`yu-cyan`) with the border turned transparent; admin, profile and form inputs use a 2px `yu-cyan-a20` ring with a `yu-cyan` border (the most common, 43 uses); Button uses `focus-visible` 2px ring with a 2px offset in the default ring color; a few selects use `ring-1 blue-500`. Selection: segmented buttons and option rows turn `yu-navy-a10` with `yu-navy` text and a `yu-navy` border; focused semester columns ring `yu-cyan`.

Loading: `animate-spin` on `loader-2` or the Button's inline spinner with the label 로딩중...; skeletons are `gray-100`/`gray-200` bars with `animate-pulse`; buttons swap their label to `…중...` and disable. Empty states are centered `gray-500` text with a dashed `gray-200` box when they invite a drop. Errors are `red-500`/`red-600` text, or a `red-50` box with a `red-200` border and `red-700` copy.

## Iconography

Lucide (`lucide-react` 0.563.0): 24-grid stroke icons at stroke 2, sized 16px in buttons and rows (`icon-md`), 20px in nav and headers, 24px for feature glyphs, 14px / 12px in dense chips. Ink follows text color: `gray-400` resting icon buttons, `gray-500` nav, `yu-navy` active, white on dark fills. The 34 icons the app imports are copied under `assets/Icons/` with their roles. A few hand-inlined Heroicons v1 glyphs (20-grid, filled) survive in CourseCard, SemesterColumn and the catalog's collapse chevron — keep them where they are, use Lucide for anything new. Text glyphs do icon duty: `+` on add buttons and "+N개 더 보기", `✓` before a satisfied count, `└` for sub-rows, `·` as the separator, `→` for projections, `↔` in a tooltip. No emoji in the UI.

Logos: the YU wordmark (`assets/Logos/yu-logo.svg`, inks `logo-navy` and `logo-cyan`) at 20–24px in the nav and 24px on the landing header; the white version at 48px on the auth brand panel and 24px in the footer, always with alt text 영남대학교. The product has no mark of its own: it is set in plain type as "YU 수강 플래너" (`heading-4` bold, white, beside the white logo in the footer).

## Not synced

Known source defects are noted where they live rather than fixed: dark currentColor hairlines on Card, the hero CTA's surviving gradient, dead `animate-in` classes, contrast pairs under 4.5:1.
