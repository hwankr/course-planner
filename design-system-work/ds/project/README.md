Course Planner is the design system behind **YU 수강 플래너**, a Next.js web app in which 영남대학교 students lay out courses semester by semester and track graduation requirements. It was read out of the codebase (Tailwind CSS v4 utilities, the `src/components` React components and the pages) and written down as tokens, usage rules and static renditions, so that whoever builds the next screen, admin table or marketing section matches what already ships.

The look: a serious university blue (`yu-navy`) with a cool cyan accent (`yu-cyan`), white cards on a `gray-50` frame, small quiet type, Lucide line icons, and a planner whose chips and surfaces carry meaning through fixed color pairs. Dense where students work, loud only on the landing and auth pages.

## Using this system

- **Tokens** (`tokens.json`, compiled to CSS custom properties): one light theme. Colors are Tailwind v4's own names (`gray-600`, `red-500`, `blue-100`) at their exact v4 values, plus the brand hexes the code hard-codes (`yu-navy`, `yu-blue`, `yu-cyan`, `yu-navy-hover` …), alpha steps (`yu-navy-a10`, `white-a60`), and a thin alias layer (`surface`, `ink-muted`, `line`, `focus-ring`, `danger`, and the planner's `category-*`, `status-*`, `pill-*`, `dept-*` pairs). Spacing is the 4px Tailwind scale (`space-2` = 8px, halves written `space-1-5`), radii `radius-xs` … `radius-full`, shadows `shadow-sm` … `shadow-2xl` plus the landing's `shadow-glow`, sizes for controls, icons and content columns, and durations and curves for motion. Every token's usage note says where the app uses it and names the grounds a text color reads on.
- **Type** is one family: the body stack `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` (the product overrides Geist on `body`); Geist and Geist Mono are declared for `font-sans` / `font-mono` but effectively unused. Styles are named by role: `display-*` (landing and auth panel), `heading-1…4`, `body`, `body-sm`, `body-sm-medium`, `caption`, `micro-11/10/9`, `overline`, `mono-xs`.
- **Components** are static renditions, not the React code: each card's `preview.html` is plain HTML styled by `components/bundle.css` (`.cp-<component>` classes; every value that has a token is written `var(--token, literal)`, while layout keywords and sizes with no token stay literal), with the component's real props in `components/index.d.ts` and usage rules in its README. Build in React with the app's own components when you are inside the repository; use the CSS and markup when you are not. Card groups: Primitives (`Button`, `Card`, `Input`, `Toast`, `SearchableSelect`, `Footer`, `ZoomableImage`, `ErrorBoundary`), Planner (`CourseCard`, `SemesterColumn`, `ReadOnlySemesterColumn`, `RequirementsSummary`, `RequirementForm`, `CustomCourseForm`, `AddSemesterDialog`, `CourseCatalog`, `AllPlansModal`, `AnonymousPlanModal`, `PatchNotePreview`) and Patterns — recurring markup the app repeats without a component (`AppHeader`, `AuthLayout`, `PageHeader`, `FilterChips`, `Badge`, `StatusStates`, `Statistics`, `Onboarding`, `CalendarGrid`, `AdminList`, `FeedbackCard`, `ProfileCard`, `PatchNotes`, `HelpSection`, `ModalShell`, `Callout`).
- **Assets**: the YU wordmark in color and white (`assets/Logos`), the 57 Lucide icons the app imports (`assets/Icons`), the PWA and social icons (`assets/App Icons`), and the help-page screenshots of the real UI (`assets/Screenshots`). Each group's README says how it is used.
- **Sections** beyond this book: Voice and copy, Planner semantics, Motion, Landing page, Layout and shell.

## Content fundamentals

Korean, polite, brief, one student at a time. Three registers, chosen by what the text does: **labels** are terse nouns or -하기 forms (바로 시작하기, 더 보기, 취소); **guidance** is 해요체 imperative (과목을 드래그하세요, 졸업 요건을 설정하세요); **statements** — status, errors, confirmations, legal — are 합니다체 (문제가 발생했습니다, 정말 삭제하시겠습니까?). The reader is never 당신; ownership is 나만의. Full sentences end with a period; labels, headings, hints and validation lines do not. Exclamation marks are rare (복사됨!, one statistics banner) and emoji do not belong in the UI (the source has two strays: 🔴 on calendar holidays, ⚠️ in a terms heading). Numbers glue to their units (3학점, 1학년 1학기, 2026년, 45개) and ` · ` separates summary items. Loanwords are Hangul (드래그앤드롭, 리스트, 커스텀); English is reserved for the product name, the slogan "YU the Future", the landing eyebrows SERVICE / HOW IT WORKS, the Focus badge, the help pages' Contact and STEP pills, and the footer's headings, Contact line and copyright. Category and status words are fixed vocabulary (전공핵심 … 자유선택; 계획 / 수강중 / 완료 / 재수강, 예정 / 이수 in compact pills). The Voice and copy section has the full rules with the product's real strings.

## Visual foundations

### Color

The brand is three blues on white. `yu-navy` (#153974) is the identity color: the help, legal and calendar page titles, links, active nav items, the Focus badge, and solid fills that always carry white text. `yu-blue` (#3069b3) is its companion: the second stop of the navy-to-blue brand gradients (`linear-gradient(to right in oklab, yu-navy, yu-blue)` on the primary Button; `to bottom right` on avatars and onboarding step circles), the info Toast rail and the "before" bar of progress deltas. `yu-cyan` (#00aaca) is the accent: focus rings, the curriculum-year badge, help-link color, decorative dots and rings, and the last stop of the footer's gradient bar — never body text (2.8:1 on white). Hover darkens: `yu-navy` → `yu-navy-hover`, gradients shift `yu-navy-hover → yu-navy`. The landing page goes deeper with `yu-navy-deepest` → `yu-navy-dark` gradients, the auth brand panel runs `yu-navy-hover` → `yu-navy` → `yu-blue`, and the footer sits on `footer-navy`.

Neutrals are Tailwind gray on the planner, profile, statistics, calendar and shell, Tailwind slate on the admin, help and patch-notes pages; the app mixes them and so may you, but keep one family within a component. The frame behind cards is `gray-50`; cards, panels, inputs and modals are `white`. Text steps: `gray-900` titles, `gray-800` strong copy and course names, `gray-700` body and labels, `gray-600` secondary copy, `gray-500` helper text (4.8:1 — the floor for body text), `gray-400` placeholders and resting icons only (2.6:1). The same ladder exists in slate (`slate-700` names, `slate-600` body, `slate-500` meta, `slate-400` icons).

Status colors are Tailwind families used as fixed pairs: a 50 or 100 tint behind 700 (or 800) text, a 500 or 600 solid under white. Danger is red (`red-600` fills, `red-500` validation text and error borders, `red-50`/`red-200` boxes); warning is amber (`amber-50` surfaces, `amber-200` borders, `amber-800` text, `amber-500` rail); success is green for enrollment and emerald for completion and positive stats; info is `blue-50` behind `blue-600` or `gray-700` copy. Requirement categories, course statuses and catalog filters have their own fixed maps — see the Planner semantics section and use the `category-*`, `status-*`, `pill-*` and `dept-*` aliases so a chip's meaning travels with its color.

Known pairs that miss 4.5:1 and are kept from the source, flagged in their token notes: `red-500` text on white (3.8:1), `amber-600` and `orange-600` on white, `emerald-600` on white (3.7:1, on the 18px total but also on 10–12px counts and chips), `gray-400` and `slate-400` as readable text, white on the 500-step filter chips (purple, orange, emerald, red, rose, blue, green, violet), `gray-500` fine print on `footer-navy`, and the `yu-cyan` focus ring on white (2.8:1). Prefer `red-600`, `amber-700`, `gray-500` and `yu-blue` in new work.

### Type

One family: the body stack is `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` (globals.css sets it on `body` after Tailwind's preflight, overriding Geist). Geist and Geist Mono are loaded through `next/font` and exposed as `font-sans` / `font-mono`, but no element opts into `font-sans`; `font-mono` appears once, on admin user IDs. Korean text therefore falls back to the OS Korean sans (Malgun Gothic, Apple SD Gothic Neo, Noto Sans CJK); do not add a webfont to "fix" this — it is the product's look.

Sizes are Tailwind's: `body-sm` (14/20) is the UI default for inputs, rows, labels and buttons, at 500 weight for anything clickable (`body-sm-medium`); `caption` (12/16) for meta, helper text and chips; `body` (16/24) only for prose, with `body-relaxed` (1.625) on help and legal pages. Headings: `heading-2` (24/32, 700) is the dashboard page title (and, at 600, CardTitle); `heading-3` (20/28, 600) error-page titles (and, at 700, statistics section titles); `heading-4` (18/28, 600) modal titles, section titles in cards and semester columns; `heading-1` (30/36, 700) the onboarding title and landing section titles on mobile. Display sizes 36–72px, bold, belong to the landing (hero and, from `md`, section titles, with `tracking-tight`) and the auth brand-panel headline; help and terms titles reach 36px at `md`. Below 12px the planner uses `micro-11`, `micro-10` and `micro-9` for chips, credit counts and the compact status pill — keep them for dense planner rows and never for required reading. Uppercase tracked eyebrows mark landing sections (`body-sm`, 0.2em) and footer column headings (`overline`, 0.1em).

### Spacing and layout

A 4px grid. Inline rhythm is `space-2` (8px gap) between items and `space-1-5` inside chips; `space-3` × `space-2` pads inputs and small buttons (12 × 8); `space-4` × `space-2` the default Button; cards pad `space-4` (compact), `space-5` or `space-6` (Card parts); modals pad `space-6` horizontally and `space-4` vertically in headers, `space-6` in bodies (`space-4` vertically in the plan modals). Vertical rhythm between blocks is `space-4` (16), between sections `space-6` (24), from page header to content `space-8` (32). Landing sections breathe at `space-16`–`space-32`.

Content columns: the nav, `<main>` and planner use Tailwind's breakpoint `container`; `container-7xl` (1280) for statistics and the calendar, `container-6xl` (1152) for the landing feature rows, `container-5xl` (1024) for help and the landing steps (`container-3xl` for feedback and legal), `container-2xl` (672) for patch notes, profile and onboarding, `container-md` (448) for auth and small modals, `container-4xl` (896) for the plan modals. Side gutters are `space-4` (the shell's `<main>` starts at `space-3`), widening from `md` only on help (`space-6`) and the calendar (`space-8`). Breakpoints are Tailwind's (`sm` 640, `md` 768, `lg` 1024); the app designs mobile-first and switches most layouts at `md` (128 uses) with a lighter `sm` tier (67).

### Shape, borders and elevation

Radii: `radius-lg` (8px) is the container radius — Card, Toast, banners, list rows; `radius-md` (6px) for controls — Button, Input, selects, segmented buttons; `radius-sm` (4px) for chips; `radius-xl` (12px) for the plan modals and the boxes inside help cards; `radius-2xl` (16px) for help section cards, admin modals and admin list boxes; `radius-full` for pills, avatars, progress bars and icon buttons; `radius-xs` (2px) for legend swatches.

Borders are 1px. Note what Tailwind v4 does with a bare `border`: it draws in `currentColor`, so Card, the semester columns, the default status pill and several `border-t` dividers really are `gray-900` hairlines — visibly dark, and part of the product's look (`border-default`). Where a light line is intended the source says so: `slate-200` on admin, help and patch-note cards and inputs, `gray-200` on Toast and catalog panels, `gray-300` on inputs, `gray-100` on read-only rows, `border-2 dashed gray-200` for drop zones, `border-2` solid for segmented buttons and selectable plan cards. Colored 4px rails (`border-l-4`) mark Toast type, answered feedback items and the calendar's selected-event card; `border-t-4` marks the onboarding step cards.

Shadows are quiet: `shadow-sm` at rest on Card and the primary Button, `shadow-md` on hover, `shadow-lg` on floating menus, Toast and the drag overlay, `shadow-xl` on modal panels, `shadow-2xl` on the two plan modals. The landing adds a navy `shadow-glow` under its CTA. The glass utility (`.glass`: white at 70% with a 12px blur) exists for the landing's sticky nav only.

### States and focus

Hover: fills step one shade (`gray-100` → `gray-200`, `yu-navy` → `yu-navy-hover`), outline buttons wash `gray-50`, rows wash `gray-50` or `slate-100`, links turn `yu-cyan` (footer) or `yu-navy` (help links), icon buttons go `gray-400` → `gray-600`, or `red-500` / `amber-500` for destructive and reset actions. Active: Button scales to 0.98. Disabled: `opacity-50` with `pointer-events: none` (Button) or `cursor-not-allowed` (Input, segmented buttons); catalog items already in the plan drop to `opacity-60` or `opacity-50`.

Focus comes in four recipes, all `outline: none` plus a ring: Input and SearchableSelect use a solid 2px `focus-ring` (`yu-cyan`) with the border turned transparent; admin and help-feedback inputs use a 2px `yu-cyan-a20` ring with a `yu-cyan` border (the most common, 43 uses); Button uses `focus-visible` 2px ring with a 2px offset in the default ring color (the error pages use the same offset ring in `yu-cyan` on `:focus`); the calendar form and a few selects use `ring-2` / `ring-1` `blue-500`. Selection: segmented buttons and option rows turn `yu-navy-a10` with `yu-navy` text and a `yu-navy` border; focused semester columns ring `yu-cyan`.

Loading: `animate-spin` on `loader-2`, on a bordered ring or arc, or on the Button's inline spinner with the label 로딩중...; skeletons are `slate-200` blocks (admin, patch notes) or `gray-100`/`gray-200` bars with `animate-pulse`; buttons swap their label to `…중...` and disable. Empty states are centered `gray-500` or `slate-500` text, `gray-400` inside a dashed `gray-200` box when they invite a drop. Errors are `red-500`/`red-600` text, or a `red-50` box with a `red-200` border and `red-600` copy.

## Iconography

Lucide (`lucide-react` 0.563.0): 24-grid stroke icons at stroke 2, sized 16px in buttons, rows and nav links (`icon-md`), 20px in headers, 24px for feature glyphs, 14px / 12px in dense chips. Ink follows text color: `gray-400` resting icon buttons, `gray-600` nav, `yu-navy` active, white on dark fills. The 57 icons the app imports are copied under `assets/Icons/` with their roles. A few hand-inlined Heroicons v1 glyphs survive (20-grid filled in CourseCard, SemesterColumn, Toast's close button, the 학기 추가 button and the catalog's collapse chevron; 24-grid outline on the error screens, SearchableSelect's clear button, RequirementsSummary's 요건 수정 and the terms back link) — keep them where they are, use Lucide for anything new. Text glyphs do icon duty: `+` on add buttons and "+N개 더 보기", `✓` before a satisfied count, `└` for sub-rows, `·` as the separator, `→` for projections, `↔` in a tooltip. No emoji in the UI.

Logos: the YU wordmark (`assets/Logos/yu-logo.svg`, inks `logo-navy` and `logo-cyan`) at 20–24px in the nav and 24px on the landing header; the white version at 48px on the auth brand panel and 24px in the footer, always with alt text 영남대학교. The product has no mark of its own: it is set in plain type as "YU 수강 플래너" (`heading-4` bold, white, beside the white logo in the footer).

## Not synced

Built from `hwankr/course-planner` at `013d3bc` (branch `claude/design-system-extraction-t0lw70`). Components are static renditions hand-translated from the React sources; the React library itself was not bundled (the app is a Next.js site, not a package, and its components depend on Next, dnd-kit, TanStack Query and Zustand). Not reproduced: drag-and-drop behavior, data fetching and store logic, focus traps, the typewriter headline. Fonts: Geist and Geist Mono are Google-hosted through `next/font` and have no files here; the body renders in Arial / Helvetica and the OS Korean fallback, as in the product. Tokens skipped: none — every CSS custom property (`--background`, `--foreground`) and hard-coded hex in `src/` is listed; Tailwind palette steps the app never uses are omitted. Every card, pattern or component, was verified line by line against the source by an independent reviewer and rendered against the app screenshots; the bundle was then audited against the CSS Tailwind 4.1.18 actually generates (sibling spacing in v4's `:not(:last-child)` form, hover only under `@media (hover: hover)`, the v4 transition lists). Contrast figures are computed (WCAG 2.x), not estimated, and these section docs and every token note were fact-checked against the source. Known source defects are noted where they live rather than fixed: dark currentColor hairlines on Card, the hero CTA's surviving gradient, dead `animate-in` classes, contrast pairs under 4.5:1.
