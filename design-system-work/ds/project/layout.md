# Layout and shell

## The dashboard shell

Every signed-in page sits inside `src/app/(dashboard)/layout.tsx`: a `gray-50` page with the faint `.bg-grid` pattern, a 4px brand gradient bar across the very top (`linear-gradient(to right in oklab, yu-navy, yu-blue, yu-cyan)`), then a white 64px header (`shadow-sm`, bare `border-b` → `border-default`), then `<main>` in a centered container with `space-3`/`space-4` gutters and `space-4`/`space-8` vertical padding (phone / `sm`). The header is not sticky; the planner compensates with a 72px scroll margin (4px bar + 64px header + 4px) when it scrolls the catalog into view. The fixed Toast stack is rendered after `<main>`.

Header row, left to right (`container`, `space-4` gutters, `h-16`, `gap-2`):

- Brand link: the color logo at 20px (24px from `md`) beside "YU 수강 플래너" in `.text-gradient`, bold, `body-sm` → `body` (`sm`) → `heading-3` size (`md`), truncating.
- Desktop nav (`md` and up, `gap-1`): pills of `body-sm-medium`, `space-2` × `space-3` padding, `radius-md`, a 16px Lucide icon and the label (수강 계획 `calendar`, 학사 일정 `calendar-days`, 학과 통계 `bar-chart-3`). Active (exact path): `yu-navy-a10` fill, `yu-navy` text, weight 600. Inactive: `gray-600`, hover `gray-50` fill and `gray-900` text. The admin link (`shield`, 관리자, prefix match) goes `purple-50` / `purple-600` when active. 더보기 opens a `w-44` menu (`chevron-down` rotates) listing 도움말, 문의 및 건의, 업데이트 소식.
- Right cluster (`gap-2` → `gap-3`): the notification bell (`bell` 20px, `gray-700` when unread, `gray-400` otherwise, in a `radius-full` hover well) with a count badge (min 18px, `micro-10` bold, `red-500`/white for students, `yellow-400`/`yellow-900` for admins, "99+" cap); the user button — a 32px avatar circle in `linear-gradient(to bottom right in oklab, yu-navy, yu-blue)` with the white initial, the name in `body-sm` `gray-600` (hidden under `sm`, max 100px), a `chevron-down` — opening a `w-48` menu with the name header, 프로필 and 로그아웃; guests get two `sm` Buttons instead (ghost 구글 로그인, default 회원가입); below `md` a `menu` / `x` toggle at 24px.
- Dropdown menus share one recipe: `white`, `radius-lg`, `shadow-lg`, bare `border`, `space-1` vertical padding, `z-modal`, items `space-2-5` × `space-4`, `body-sm`, 16px icon, active items `yu-navy-a10` + `yu-navy` + weight 600, hover `gray-50`. The notification panel is the same at 320px (`sm:w-80`; full-width fixed with `space-2` side insets on phones), with an 알림 header, a `모두 읽음` `caption-medium` link in `yu-cyan` → `yu-navy`, rows of a 32px tinted icon circle (`purple-100`/`purple-600` `megaphone` for updates, `yellow-100`/`yellow-600` `message-square-text` for new feedback, `blue-100`/`blue-600` otherwise), `body-sm` `gray-700` message and a `caption` `gray-400` relative time (방금 전 · N분 전 · N시간 전 · N일 전 · M월 D일), separated by `gray-50` hairlines, and an empty state of a 32px `gray-300` bell over 알림이 없습니다.
- Mobile menu (below `md`): a `border-t` panel fading in, items at `space-3` vertical padding, a 고객 지원 accordion for the secondary links (sub-items `gray-500`, indented `space-4`), then 프로필 and 로그아웃.
- Guest banner (when browsing without an account): a full-width `amber-50` band with an `amber-200` bottom border, `alert-triangle` 16px in `amber-800`, "비회원 모드입니다. 데이터가 이 브라우저에만 저장됩니다." in `caption` → `body-sm`, and two underlined `amber-900` links (구글로 로그인, 회원가입하기).

## Page header

Three recipes exist. The planner's: `heading-3` → `heading-2` (`sm`) bold `gray-900` title, a row of `radius-full` `caption-medium` badges (`yu-navy-a10` / `yu-navy` for the department, `purple-100` / `purple-700` for 복수전공, `orange-100` / `orange-700` for 부전공), a `body-sm` → `body` `gray-600` instruction line, and right-aligned actions (an outline Button for 내보내기 opening a `min-w-[140px]` dropdown with PNG 이미지 / PDF 문서, and an outline Button turned destructive with `red-600` text, `red-300` border and `red-50` hover for 초기화); the row stacks on phones and aligns on `sm`. The statistics page's: `heading-1` bold with the title in `.text-gradient`, a `heading-3`-size `gray-700` subtitle, and a `body-sm` `gray-500` meta line with a 16px `users` icon (N명의 학생 데이터 기반 · N분 전 갱신). The admin sub-pages': a back link (`body-sm-medium` in `yu-cyan` → `yu-navy` with `arrow-left`) above a `heading-2` title and, on three of them, a primary button on the right.

## Planner page composition

A vertical stack (`space-6`): the RequirementsSummary card; the CourseCatalog panel full-width (not a sidebar); then semesters grouped by year — a `body-sm-semibold` `gray-500` uppercase-tracked heading `{year}학년` with `(N학점)` in `caption` `gray-400`, a one-time legend (예정 ↔ 이수 클릭하여 변경 in 10px chips), and a `grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 gap-3` grid of compact SemesterColumns. A year-filter pill row (`micro-11` 학년 label, pills `yu-navy` active / `yu-navy-a10` inactive) scrolls horizontally above the grid. The last element is a centered dashed ghost Button (학기 추가 with an inline plus glyph, `border-2 dashed`, `gray-500` → `gray-700`). While a course is dragged, a 250px compact CourseCard ghost floats at `opacity-90` with `shadow-lg`. During PNG/PDF export the page forces a 768px minimum width and swaps in a compact header.

## Auth pages

Split screen from `md`: the brand panel (half, three-fifths from `lg`) runs the animated gradient with `.bg-mesh` at 30% and three blurred `white-a20` discs floating; its content is the white logo at 48px, a `display-sm` → `display-md` (`lg`) bold white headline on two lines ("영남대학교와 함께하는\n학업 계획"), and three feature bullets (`check-circle-2` 24px, `heading-3`-size titles, `blue-100` descriptions). The form panel is white with `space-8` padding and a `container-md` column: logo at 24px beside the gradient wordmark in `heading-2` size, the page title in `heading-2` bold `gray-900` with a `gray-500` subtitle, a `space-y-4` field stack (label `body-sm-medium` `gray-700`, Input), a full-width primary Button, then on login only an 또는 divider (a `gray-300` rule behind a `gray-500` label on white), an outline Google button with a monochrome G, a quiet `gray-500` → `yu-navy` text button for guests, and a centered `body-sm` footer line whose link is `yu-navy` medium with underline on hover. Errors are the inline alert: `red-50`, `red-200` border, `radius-md`, `space-3` padding, `body-sm` `red-600`.

## Status states

- Loading: three spinners coexist — the route loader (32px ring, 4px `gray-200` track with a `yu-navy` top arc, 로딩 중... in `body-sm` `gray-500`), the guard and planner loader (an arc only, 2px `yu-blue` bottom border, 32px / 48px), and the `loader-2` Lucide icon spinning in `yu-blue` (32px, statistics and AllPlansModal). Reserve space with a 400px minimum height (`min-h-[400px]`, or `50vh` for the guard).
- Skeletons: `animate-pulse` blocks in `gray-200` (a single `h-4` bar in RequirementsSummary), `gray-100` (calendar cells), `slate-100` (feedback rows) or `slate-200` at `radius-2xl` (admin lists: `h-20`–`h-32` rows; the admin auth skeleton is an `h-8 w-48` title bar over an `h-64` block).
- Error and 404 pages: a centered column (`gap-4`, `space-8` padding, 400px minimum in both `error.tsx` files, full height in the 404 and global error) — a `radius-full` `red-100` well holding a 32px `red-600` outline triangle (the 404 uses `blue-100` and a `yu-navy` sad face), a `heading-3` 600 `gray-900` heading (문제가 발생했습니다 / 페이지를 찾을 수 없습니다), `body-sm` `gray-600` copy, a dev-only `<details>` with the error message in a `gray-100` `radius-sm` `<pre>`, and a hand-rolled primary button (`yu-navy` fill, `yu-navy-hover`, `yu-cyan` focus ring with offset) reading 다시 시도 or 수강 플래너로 이동.
- Empty states: in lists, centered `gray-500` text with a medium-weight `body` first line and `body-sm` second line (아직 공개된 수강 계획이 없습니다 / 학과 학생들이 …); in admin, a white `radius-2xl` box with a `slate-200` border, `space-12` padding, a 48px `slate-300` icon and one `body-sm` `slate-500` sentence; in the planner, the dashed `gray-200` drop zone; on the statistics page, a centered Card (`container-2xl`, `space-8` padding) with a 64px icon (`gray-400` chart, `red-500` alert, or a `yu-blue` info glyph in a 64px `blue-100` circle), a `heading-2` bold heading and, when recoverable, a gradient CTA.

## Admin conventions

Admin pages (`/admin/*`) are the slate side of the product: `space-y-6` stacks of row Cards instead of tables (`CardContent` at `space-4` → `space-5`, a truncating title column with `caption-medium` badges, a `caption` `slate-400` meta line, and a right-aligned cluster of 16px icon buttons: edit `slate-400` → `slate-600` on `slate-100`, delete → `red-600`). Above them a filter bar (`flex-wrap gap-3`) of a search field — `search` icon absolutely placed at `left-3`, input padded `pl-9`/`pl-10`, an optional clear `x` — and native selects in the shared soft recipe (`radius-lg`, `slate-200` border, `yu-cyan-a20` focus ring). Primary buttons are `yu-cyan` filled hovering to `yu-navy` on courses and departments, `yu-navy` filled hovering to `yu-navy-darker` / `yu-blue-dark-alt` on patch notes and requirements; cancel buttons come in four shades of slate. Destructive confirmation takes five forms (inline [삭제][취소] swap, inline [확인][취소], a `container-sm` confirm modal, native `confirm()`, an expanded footer warning) — pick the inline swap for row actions and the modal for records with dependents. The admin dashboard is a 2 → 4 column grid of stat Cards whose numbers are `heading-1` bold in `yu-navy`, `green-600`, `purple-600` and `orange-600`, plus a 1 → 2 column grid of menu Cards.

## Calendar

A 7-column grid (`gap-0`, `gap-1` from `md`) under a weekday row where 일 is `red-600` and 토 `blue-600`. Day cells are 90px tall on phones and square from `md`, `gray-200` bordered (`radius-lg` from `md`), white with `gray-50` hover; today is `blue-50` with a `blue-400` (2px `blue-500` from `md`) border; a selected day is `amber-50-a50` with an `amber-300` border on phones and white with a 2px `amber-400` border on desktop; today-and-selected combines the blue fill with an `amber-400` ring. Events are `caption` chips in the academic-event colors (학사 `blue-100`/`blue-700`/`blue-200`, 수강신청 green, 시험 red, 공휴일 rose, 기타 gray), reduced to `micro-10` tinted text on phones, with a `+N` overflow counter in `gray-500` (`gray-400` on phones); holidays carry a 🔴 prefix in the source. The 전체 filter chip is `blue-600` when active (bordered `gray-300` white otherwise), unlike the brand pills elsewhere.
